What ThePickLog is

ThePickLog is a transparency-first research lab operated by AMD Ventures, LLC. We publish quantitative hypotheses about the stock market, test them out-of-sample, and log the results — including the ones that fail. The site exists so a stranger can verify our claims, not so anyone can copy trades.

Everything shown is research, not a signal

Screener output, graded picks, and hypothesis results are published as general-interest research. They are not real-time "act now" alerts. Market data may be delayed. Do not treat any list on this site as a set of trades to place.

Simulated performance

All performance, positions, and the track record are generated from simulated (paper) trading using historical or delayed data. No real money is at risk, and simulated results do not reflect the costs, slippage, liquidity limits, or emotions of real trading.
