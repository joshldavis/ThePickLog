How the score works

No "proprietary AI." The IgnitionScan score is a transparent, deterministic blend of four objective inputs. Publishing it is deliberate — it builds trust and keeps the product on the educational side of the line.

The formula

Component weights

What each input means

Float — shares available to trade. Smaller float, more violent moves. Sweet spot under ~5M shares.

RVOL — today's volume vs. the stock's own average. The single best tell that something unusual is happening. 5×+ is notable.

Gap % — move from prior close into the pre-market. Bigger gaps = more attention and momentum.

Price band — strategy targets roughly $0.50–$10. Outside the band is down-weighted, not auto-excluded.

Tiers: A ≥ 75 · B ≥ 60 · C ≥ 45 · D below. Watch level = entry reference set at +20% from the screen price; "WIN" = the stock reaches it within 5 trading days.

The methodology is provided for education. A high score is not a recommendation to buy; it is a description of how a stock ranks on these public criteria.
