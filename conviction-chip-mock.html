Conviction chip — pick & rule card

Fynca tags every news item "Why Bullish / Bearish" with a confidence read. We do the same to picks and rules — except the tag states the rule's registered claim and the confidence chip is driven by ThePickLog's real out-of-sample math, not sentiment. Nothing here is decorative; every chip re-derives from picks.csv × outcomes.csv.

How to read a card — data mapping
  • Directional tag = the rule's claim type from the registry: Filter · helps Filter · hurts Exit rule Question · no claim. A Question is never colored as good/bad — it has no claim to rank.
  • Why line = one plain-English sentence stating what the rule bets and why it's worth testing. Written at registration, frozen.
  • Field chips = the pick-time conditions only (tier, float_shares, gap_pct, rvol, short_interest_pct, exit). No outcome fields — nothing that references the future.
  • Δ vs baseline = mean net-return edge per trade vs the same-day-close baseline, with its 95% bootstrap CI.
  • Confidence chip is computed, not chosen: ✓ Proven CI clears zero, n≥30, sign stable ≥3 wks · ~ Directional CI still spans zero (ns) · ○ Maturing n<30 · ± Mixed sign flips week to week.
